HOW AUSTRALIA MOVES

Five decades of public transport across Australia's capital cities

Australia's capital cities do not move in the same way. This visualisation compares where public transport use is highest, how usage changes after population is considered, how trains, trams, buses and ferries shape each city's transport system, and how patronage has changed over time.

01 — WHERE AUSTRALIA MOVES

Public transport patronage varies substantially between Australia's capital cities. Raw totals reveal the largest markets, while population-adjusted measures provide a different perspective on how intensively residents use public transport.

TOTAL PATRONAGE

CITY RANKING

Ranking the same 2024–25 patronage figures makes the gap between Australia's largest and smaller public transport markets easier to compare precisely.

PER RESIDENT

Does city size change the picture?

Total patronage partly reflects population size. This tile-grid map compares public transport trips per resident, giving each capital equal visual space while approximately preserving geographic position. Darker tiles indicate more trips per resident in 2024–25.

PER-RESIDENT RANKING

Which capitals use public transport most intensively?

Comparing trips per resident reduces the influence of population size. The lollipop ranking makes the differences between capitals easier to compare, while the dashed reference line shows the average across all eight cities.

02 — EVERY CITY MOVES DIFFERENTLY

Public transport systems are not structured the same way across Australia. Some cities depend heavily on trains, while others rely much more strongly on buses, trams, light rail or ferries.

DOMINANT MODE

Which transport mode dominates each capital?

Equal-sized symbols give every capital the same visual importance, while colour identifies the mode carrying the largest number of passenger trips in 2024–25.

TRANSPORT MIX

Every city has a different transport mix

Dominant mode tells only part of the story. This heatmap compares the percentage of each city's public transport patronage carried by trains, trams and light rail, buses and ferries. Darker cells indicate a larger share of that city's total trips.

NATIONAL MODE SHARE

Trains and buses carry most public transport trips

Looking across all eight capital cities, trains and buses account for most public transport patronage. Each square represents approximately one percentage point of passenger trips in 2024–25. Hover over the squares to view the exact share and passenger total.

Key takeaway: Train and bus travel together account for the large majority of capital-city public transport patronage.

03 — FIVE DECADES ON THE MOVE

Public transport use has changed substantially over almost five decades. Long-term growth has been interrupted by periods of stagnation and, most dramatically, the COVID-19 disruption.
